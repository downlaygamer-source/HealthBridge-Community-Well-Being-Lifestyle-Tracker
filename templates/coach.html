UN SDG 3 · Good Health & Well-Being

Target 3.4 — Preventative Lifestyle Reflections

Sedentary habits, chronic sleep deficit, and poor hydration are preventable drivers of cardiovascular strain, metabolic fatigue, and cognitive burnout. HealthBridge provides transparent daily tracking to empower preventative habits before clinical intervention is needed.

UN SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities

Target 10.2 — Accessible, Barrier-Free Habits

Modern commercial wellness apps demand expensive wearable sensors, gym memberships, or subscription meal kits. HealthBridge focuses exclusively on zero-cost habits (tap water, neighborhood walking, sleep regularity) accessible to individuals of any economic background.
